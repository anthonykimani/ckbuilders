# Fiber Digital-Service Payment Interview Guide

Target: 5 buyers and 5 providers of small digital services.

## Screening

1. What digital service do you buy or sell?
2. How often do payments occur?
3. What is the usual payment size and currency?
4. Which country is the buyer in and which country is the provider in?

## Current workflow

5. How is payment made today?
6. What fees, delays, minimums or failed-payment problems occur?
7. Who pays the transaction or conversion fee?
8. How are refunds and disputes handled?

## Micropayment fit

9. Would smaller pay-per-use payments change how the service is sold?
10. At what payment size do existing methods become uneconomic?
11. Is instant settlement valuable, or is daily/weekly settlement sufficient?
12. Would the participant hold a digital asset temporarily?
13. Which matters more: stable fiat value, low fees, privacy or settlement speed?

## Validation

14. Ask for one recent concrete payment example.
15. Present the Fiber concept without claiming production readiness.
16. Ask what would prevent the participant from trying it.
17. Ask whether they would join a testnet or small-value pilot.

## Evidence to record

- role: buyer or provider;
- service category;
- frequency;
- typical amount and currency;
- current method and effective fee;
- strongest pain point;
- willingness to try;
- blocking concern;
- permission to quote anonymously.

Do not record names, wallet addresses or contact details in the public dataset without explicit permission.
