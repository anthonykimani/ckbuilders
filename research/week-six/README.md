# Week 6 Research Dataset

The CSV is a conservative snapshot of projects and infrastructure discussed publicly in the CKB ecosystem up to 22 September 2026.

## Classification rules

- CKB/Fiber dependency asks whether the project materially depends on the Cell Model, CKB-VM, RGB++ or Fiber.
- Public evidence records what could be verified from the cited public source.
- Maturity is not a security endorsement. A released or runnable project may still be experimental or unaudited.
- Main gap identifies the next unanswered question, not a claim that the team has failed.

This is a purposive sample rather than a complete census. Forum activity favors projects whose authors publish on Nervos Talk, and private development is not visible.
