# CKB Builders — Projects 1 and 2

## 1. CellEscrow

A cell-native conditional payment protocol. It demonstrates binary cell-data design, claim/refund branches, timelocks, output constraints, live-cell discovery and adversarial validation.

Path: [`cell-escrow/`](cell-escrow/README.md)

## 2. Fiber Invoice Lab

A provider-driven Fiber checkout core with deterministic local simulation and a real-SDK adapter boundary. It demonstrates invoice lifecycle design, expiry, idempotency and the separation between application UX and payment-channel infrastructure.

Path: [`fiber-invoice-lab/`](fiber-invoice-lab/README.md)

Together, the projects compare two complementary CKB application modes: verifiable settlement on Layer 1 and fast payment sessions over Fiber.
